About

I'm a Full-Stack Engineer & AI Researcher based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, pursuing a B.Eng. in Computer Science & Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET).

I specialize in LLM systems, RAG pipelines, and hallucination reduction with production integrations across Anthropic, OpenAI, and Gemini APIs. My thesis work on CertiMark-RAG — a hybrid-uncertainty watermarking framework for retrieval-augmented generation — explores token-confidence metrics and 34-feature ensemble detection for tamper-evident LLM output.

I've placed 7th worldwide at ACL 2025 CQs-Gen (ArgMining Workshop, Vienna) and 21st at NAACL 2025 in competitive NLP shared tasks, authored 3 publications (ACL, IEEE ICCIT, SPICSCON), and built production systems spanning OIDC auth services, distributed file processing with Ray, and real-time fintech platforms.
